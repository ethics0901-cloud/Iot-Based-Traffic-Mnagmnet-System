MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION

The Max-Pressure Algorithm & Historical Blending

Decentralized physics-inspired queue control that maximizes intersection throughput.

ALGORITHM 04
1. Instantaneous Sensor Pressure

At any time $t$, each approach lane calculates vehicle density:

P_{\text{live}} = \frac{\text{Vehicles Waiting (Count)}}{\text{Distance Monitored (Meters)}}

Example: Road 1 with 50 cars in 100m has $P_1 = 0.50\,\text{veh/m}$. Road 2 with 50 cars spread over 500m has $P_2 = 0.10\,\text{veh/m}$. Traditional timers treat them equally; Max-Pressure gives green to Road 1.

2. Hybrid Adaptive Historical Blending

To prevent erratic signal flipping from momentary sensor blips, we blend live readings with historical priors from Supabase:

P_{\text{eff}} = (1 - w) \cdot P_{\text{live}} + w \cdot \bar{P}_{\text{hist}}
Where $w$ is the confidence weight ($0.0 \le w \le 1.0$). Dynamic green phase durations are sized proportionally: $$T_{\text{green}} = T_{\text{min}} + \left(\frac{P_1}{P_1 + P_2}\right) \cdot (T_{\text{max}} - T_{\text{min}})$$ Yielding safe, smoothed phase clearance windows ($15\text{s} - 60\text{s}$).

Emergency Vehicle Priority Wave (EV-Corridor)

Amber Clearance Flush

Opposing road is immediately forced to AMBER, safely giving drivers crossing distance and preventing rear-end collisions.

All-Red Safety Buffer

PHASE 03 (10s – 20s)
Green Wave Corridor

Emergency corridor locked in continuous GREEN with pulsing strobe light aura; opposing lane locked RED; siren audio activated.
